**Step 1 — Scene type identification:**

**Step 2 — Environment and setting:**
- Interior or exterior?
- Time of day (if determinable): dawn, morning, midday, golden hour, blue hour, night
- Weather conditions (if exterior): clear, overcast, fog, rain, snow, haze, storm
- Season (if determinable): spring, summer, autumn, winter
- Geographic or architectural context: urban, rural, forest, desert, coastal, mountain, industrial, domestic, commercial, institutional
- Specific environment: street, park, beach, forest, market, office, living room, restaurant, stadium, rooftop, etc.
- Depth layers: foreground elements, midground elements, background elements, sky or ceiling

**Step 3 — Subjects and figures (if present):**
For each person or animal visible:
- Position in frame (center, left, right, foreground, background)
- Apparent gender, age range
- Pose and body language (standing, sitting, walking, running, turned, profile, etc.)
- Expression (if face visible)
- Clothing: garment types, colors, materials, condition
- Accessories: bags, hats, glasses, jewelry, tools, props
- Any text on clothing or accessories

**Step 4 — Objects and props:**
- All significant objects in the scene
- Their material, condition, color, size relative to frame
- Their spatial relationship to primary subject
- Any text, logos, or labels visible on objects

**Step 5 — Architecture and structures:**
- Building types, architectural style, era
- Materials: brick, concrete, glass, wood, metal, stone
- Condition: new, weathered, aged, maintained, decayed
- Windows, doors, facades, signage
- Any visible text on buildings, signs, storefronts

**Step 6 — Lighting:**
- Primary light source: sun, artificial lights, mixed, unknown
- Direction: frontal, side, backlit, top-down, under-lit, multiple sources
- Quality: hard/directional (sharp shadows), soft/diffuse (gentle shadows), dappled, volumetric
- Color temperature: warm (golden/amber), cool (blue/grey), neutral, mixed
- Time-of-day light quality: golden hour warmth, midday harshness, overcast flatness, blue hour coolness, night artificial
- Shadow description: direction, sharpness, length, where they fall
- Highlight areas: where light catches surfaces, specular reflections, lens flare if present
- Special lighting effects: fog/haze, god rays, neon glow, bokeh lights in background, light trails

**Step 7 — Color analysis:**
- Dominant color family of the entire image
- Color temperature of the overall scene
- Any strong color contrast or complementary color relationships
- Saturation level: desaturated/muted, natural, vivid/saturated
- Any visible color grading or toning (cinematic teal-orange, warm film, cool blue, black and white, etc.)
- Extract 8–16 dominant hex colors covering: sky/background, main subject, shadows, highlights, accent colors, ground/floor, foliage or environmental tones

**Step 8 — Composition and framing:**
- Rule of thirds, centered, symmetrical, diagonal, leading lines, frames within frames
- Camera angle: eye level, low angle, high angle, bird's eye, worm's eye, Dutch tilt
- Shot type: extreme wide, wide, medium wide, medium, medium close-up, close-up, extreme close-up, macro
- Depth of field: deep (everything sharp), shallow (subject sharp, background blurred), selective focus
- Bokeh quality if present: smooth/busy/geometric, color
- Lens characteristics: wide angle distortion, telephoto compression, tilt-shift effect, fisheye
- Any motion blur: subject motion, camera motion, long exposure trails

**Step 9 — Atmosphere and mood:**
- Overall atmospheric quality: crisp/clean, hazy/misty, smoky, dusty, humid, dry
- Any visible atmospheric particles: fog, mist, rain, snow, dust, smoke, steam, pollen
- Mood conveyed by the image (describe factually, not emotionally): high contrast drama, soft intimacy, harsh midday flatness, moody low-light, vibrant energy, quiet stillness
- Any post-processing visible: heavy grain, vignette, color shift, HDR-style, film emulation

**Step 10 — Text in image:**
- Any readable text visible anywhere in the scene: signs, storefronts, labels, clothing, graffiti, newspapers, screens, license plates, etc.
- For each: exact text content if readable, font style if determinable, color, position in frame
